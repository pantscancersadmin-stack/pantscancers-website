EARLY DETECTION SAVES LIVES

The Pants Area Cancers

Never feel embarrassed to talk to your GP. Spotting symptoms early makes all the difference. Here are the key warning signs for cancers affecting the pants area.

  • GYNAECOLOGICAL
    💜

    Womb (Uterine) Cancer

    Key Warning Sign: Any post-menopausal vaginal bleeding or unusual discharge. 4th most common cancer in UK women.

  • GYNAECOLOGICAL
    💛

    Ovarian Cancer

    Key Warning Signs: Persistent bloating, feeling full quickly, pelvic or abdominal pain, and increased urgency to wee.

  • GYNAECOLOGICAL
    💗

    Cervical Cancer

    Key Warning Signs: Unusual bleeding between periods, after sex, or after menopause. Regular NHS smear tests save lives.

  • MEN'S HEALTH
    💙

    Prostate Cancer

    Key Warning Signs: Difficulty peeing, weak flow, or needing to empty your bladder more often at night. Most common cancer in UK men.

  • MEN'S HEALTH
    💙

    Testicular Cancer

    Key Warning Signs: A painless lump or swelling in either testicle, or a feeling of heaviness in the scrotum. Check yourself monthly.

  • COLORECTAL
    💚

    Bowel Cancer

    Key Warning Signs: Bleeding from your bottom, blood in stool, or persistent changes in bowel habits lasting 3+ weeks.

OUR FOUNDER & COMMUNITY STORY

Breaking Taboos Below the Belt

Pants Cancers is an authentic registered charity raising awareness of cancers affecting the ‘pants’ area, whilst funding the CancerRehab Centre in Falmouth, Cornwall.

We operate a 6-unit Community Hub at Ponsharden and our Posh Pants shop in Falmouth town.

Pants Cancers was founded in 2007 by Jeannette Preston, an 18-year survivor of womb cancer, on a mission to eliminate embarrassment, normalize early screening, and save lives.

Situated on Ponsharden Industrial Estate since 2010, our charity now utilizes 6 full units filled with low-cost essentials, vintage clothing, and household goods — providing critical cost-of-living relief across Cornwall, the second poorest region in Northern Europe.

Beyond affordable essentials, Pants provides work experience, rehabilitation facilities, volunteering opportunities, and a warm, safe community space for survivors and families going through treatment.

Survivor's Stories!

Real stories from people supported through our CancerRehab Centre in Cornwall.

Ian

Prostate Cancer

“Steve has for several years worked in co-ordination with Cornwall Hospital Treliske in enabling a facility for those who have undergone cancer treatment. His diligence, and organisation has been second to none, and has adopted a sympathetic approach to fitness. He does not push, but advises the best fitness tasks suitable. All of this he does with a cheerful approach, and concern.”

Patrick

Prostate Cancer

“I’ve been attending the gym twice a week for nearly two years now, and the benefits are many. Improvements in my physical fitness are definitely noticeable with improvements in muscle tone and some loss of weight. The supervision and advice from Steve Winnan really helps in developing an individual exercise regime. One of the unexpected benefits has been the growth of friendships amongst regular members.”

Sid

Tonsil Cancer

“Steve prepared me a custom based programme aimed at building up muscle base and increased general fitness and the benefits are remarkable. Not only have I put on weight in a controlled manner, my muscles have toned right up. I'm now back playing golf twice a week and even winning some money. My golfing pals now refer to me as Lazarus!”
