Summer Opening Hours at the Hubs and Posh Pants!

Spring has sprung at Pants with loads of new bargains to be had! So come and visit us and continue to help support the great work Pants do for pants area cancers.

Here are our Summer Opening Hours for the Hubs at Ponsharden, Falmouth, TR112SG and Posh Pants, 5 Webber Street, Falmouth, TR113AU

 – The Hub is open every day from 9am – 5pm, except Sunday when it is open 11am – 3pm.

– Our Posh Pants shop is open 10am – 5.15pm daily and closed Bank holidays and Sundays.

* Please ring before visiting as on occasions we’ve had to close during opening hours *

We hope the re-emergence of the sun and flowers bring you joy and lift your spirits and we look forward to seeing you at Hubs or Posh Pants soon.
